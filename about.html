📄 What are the requirements for a business loan?

To qualify, your business typically needs to be operating for at least 6 months with a minimum monthly revenue of $10,000. We also review your credit profile and other factors to determine the best loan options for your business.

  • Business operating for at least 6 months
  • Minimum monthly revenue of $10,000
  • Valid business bank account
  • Review of credit profile and other factors

🕓 How long does approval take?

Our streamlined process allows for fast approvals. In most cases, you can receive a decision within 24 to 48 hours of submitting your complete application.

📄 What documents are required to apply?

We typically require business bank statements for the last 6 months, business tax returns, a valid business license, and a government-issued ID for the business owner.

💰 How much funding can I get?

Funding amounts range from $5,000 up to $100M+ depending on your business's revenue, creditworthiness, and specific funding needs.

📈 What are the interest rates and fees?

Our rates are competitive and tailored to your business profile. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Contact us for a personalized quote.

📈 How and when do I repay the loan?

Repayment schedules are flexible. We offer daily, weekly, or monthly payment options to match your business cash flow cycle.

📈 Can I pay off the loan early?

Yes, we encourage early repayment! We do not charge any prepayment penalties, so you can save on interest by paying off your loan ahead of schedule.
