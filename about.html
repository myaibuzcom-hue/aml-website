About AML Lending Group

Empowering businesses with capital since 2015.

Our Story

AML Lending Group was founded in Houston, Texas, with a singular mission: to make business capital accessible, transparent, and fast. What began as a small brokerage has grown into a trusted lending institution serving businesses across the United States.

We understand that every business is unique — whether you're launching a startup, expanding operations, or purchasing commercial property. Our team of experienced underwriters and relationship managers works personally with each client to structure financing that fits.

Today, AML Lending Group has facilitated over $100 million in business funding, helping companies create jobs and drive economic growth.

Our Mission

To provide fair, fast, and flexible financing that enables businesses to thrive. We remove barriers between ambition and achievement.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted business lending partner in America — known for transparency, integrity, and putting clients first.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, speed, and partnership. Every decision we make is guided by what's best for the businesses we serve.

Our Leadership

Mrs Rolanda Medina

Managing Director

With over 20 years of experience in commercial banking and private lending, Mrs Medina leads AML Lending Group with a passion for helping businesses succeed. She has underwritten over $500 million in loans throughout her career and believes that access to capital should never be the barrier to a great business idea.

Under her leadership, AML Lending Group has expanded from a local Houston lender to a nationally recognized business funding partner. Mrs Medina holds an MBA from the University of Texas and serves on the board of several Houston business organisations.
