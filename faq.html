Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our business loans.

What are the requirements for a business loan?

We require a completed application form, proof of business ownership, bank statements for the last 3 months, and a valid government‑issued ID. Specific requirements vary by loan type — our team will guide you.

How long does approval take?

Most applications are reviewed within 24–48 hours. Once approved, funds are disbursed within 1–3 business days, depending on the loan type.

What interest rates do you offer?

Our rates are competitive and based on the loan type, amount, and term. Contact us for a personalised quote — we pride ourselves on transparency with no hidden fees.

Can I apply if my business is less than a year old?

Yes! We work with startups and established businesses alike. For newer businesses, we may ask for a business plan or additional documentation.

How do I become a broker partner?

Visit our Brokers page and click "Get Started." Fill out the short form and our partnership team will contact you within 24 hours.

Is there a prepayment penalty?

No — we never charge prepayment penalties. You can pay off your loan early at any time with no extra cost.

How is my personal information protected?

We use industry‑standard encryption and secure database storage. Your documents are stored privately and only accessible to authorised personnel. See our Privacy Policy for details.
